# Strategic projects for Tutor Intelligence

Two growth plays for Tutor Intelligence's CPG business, prepared by Abdullah Usman. Both are served as static pages with GitHub Pages from the `docs/` folder.

- **[PACK EXPO Southeast 2027 campaign plan](https://abdullah-coder25.github.io/tutor-strategic-projects/packexpo/)** (`docs/packexpo/`): a ready-to-run plan for Atlanta, April 12 to 14, 2027. It covers target accounts, pre-show outreach emails, a three-day meeting schedule, an on-floor playbook, a follow-up tracker with CSV export, budget math and sources.
- **[Workers' comp carrier partnership](https://abdullah-coder25.github.io/tutor-strategic-projects/carrier-partnership/)** (`docs/carrier-partnership/`): a two-page brief on how carriers' risk-control consultants could refer Tutor to the plants with the most hand lifting, while Tutor keeps the direct sale.

Home page: https://abdullah-coder25.github.io/tutor-strategic-projects/

Contact names on these pages are public names and titles only; last names from Apollo's free search are masked. Funnel numbers and budgets are labeled as assumptions on each page.
