Expansion Radar · PACK EXPO Southeast 2027 plan · Carrier partnership one-pager
Prepared for Tutor Intelligence Abdullah Usman · November 2026

Who already pays for the lifting Tutor automates? Workers' comp carriers.

Overexertion is the costliest cause of serious U.S. workplace injury, and carriers already send risk-control consultants into CPG plants and co-packers. A referral and co-funding program with two or three carriers would bring Tutor warm introductions to the plants with the most hand lifting, while Tutor keeps the direct sale.
